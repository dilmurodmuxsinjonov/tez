## 🎨 Google Imagen 3 Professional Prompt Standarti

Imagen 3 ning mikro-teksturalar va fotorealizm imkoniyatlarini maksimal ochish uchun quyidagi 4 blokli formuladan foydalaniladi:

```
[1. Asosiy Obyekt & Harakat] + [2. Optika & Kamera] + [3. Yoritish Uslubi] + [4. Rang & Render Sifati]
```

Shot on Hasselblad H6D-100c with 85mm f/1.4 lens, soft volumetric daylight filtering through large floor-to-ceiling glass windows, cinematic depth of field, ultra-realistic skin pores and natural fabric textures, 8k resolution photorealistic color grading."*

## 🎥 Google Veo: Kinematik Video Generatsiyasi

Veo modeli 1080p sifatdagi barqaror va dinamik sahnalarni yaratadi.

### Veo Prompt Standarti:
```
[Kamera Harakati] ➡️ [Ob'yektning Dinamika Harakati] ➡️ [Muhit & Atmosfera] ➡️ [Kino Uslubi]
```

- **Kamera harakatlari:** `Smooth drone tracking shot`, `Slow cinematic pan right`, `Low-angle orbit shot`, `First-person dolly forward`.
- **Vaqt va atmosfera:** `Golden hour sunset rays`, `Drizzle rain with reflections on asphalt`, `Neon haze`.

## 🎧 Google MusicFX / Lyria: Dinamik Saundtrek Generatsiyasi

- **DJ Mode**: Temp (BPM), asboblar va janrlarni real vaqtda mikslash.
- **Prompt formulasi:** `[Janr: Lo-fi ambient chillhop] + [BPM: 82 BPM] + [Asboblar: Rhodes piano, warm vinyl crackle, subtle acoustic guitar] + [Kayfiyat: Peaceful, deep concentration, coding focus]`.
